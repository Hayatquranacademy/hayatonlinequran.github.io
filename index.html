Online Quran Courses for Kids & Adults

Structured online Quran classes for children and adults, taught one-on-one and paced to each student’s level.

For Children

Nazra & Hifz

Reading fluency, correct pronunciation, and full or partial memorisation, at a pace suited to the child.

  • Qaida & Nazra (reading with correct Tajweed)
  • Hifz (memorisation) with daily revision plan

Tajweed & Tarjuma

Correct your recitation and build a real understanding of the Qur'an's meaning.

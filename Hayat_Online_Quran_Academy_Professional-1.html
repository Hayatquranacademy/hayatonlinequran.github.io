About your teacher

Sikandar Hayat — Hafiz-e-Qur'an

I am Sikandar Hayat, a certified Hafiz-e-Qur'an, having completed my Hifz and religious education under the Wifaq-ul-Madaris Pakistan. I am also an Aalim-e-Deen, with a solid grounding in Islamic studies alongside my Qur'anic training.

For over 12 years, I have taught the Holy Qur'an to students of all ages — helping young children build a strong foundation in Nazra and Hifz, and guiding adults toward correct Tajweed and a deeper understanding of the Qur'an through Tarjuma.
